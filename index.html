Hello! I'm Mónica Ulloa.

I'm an anthropologist with a Master's degree in Social Studies of Science. My work looks at how emerging technologies shape the resilience and flourishing of societies.

Doings

I currently teach ethics workshops at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia. Separately, I'm working on an independent project about social cohesion under advanced AI (draft here). I also play drums in a band, mostly playing Fugazi covers, and acoustic guitar on my own.
